How to use it
  1. Tap Start camera and allow access.
  2. Pick a sign (a letter) from the list and hold that hand shape to the camera.
  3. Tap Record sample 5–10 times for that sign. Repeat for other signs.
  4. Now hold any taught sign steady for a moment — it is typed into your sentence.
  5. Tap Speak to read it aloud.

Everything runs on your phone. Your samples are saved in the browser, so they stay after a refresh. Recognition needs a good light and the whole hand in view.
